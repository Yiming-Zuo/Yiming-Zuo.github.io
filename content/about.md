## About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Artificial Intelligence at the [School of Computer Science and Technology](https://it.ouc.edu.cn/), [Ocean University of China](https://www.ouc.edu.cn/). I am a member of the Interdisciplinary AI Lab, advised by [Professor Ruichun Tang](https://tangruichun.github.io/).

My research asks how learned transport between complex molecular distributions can make molecular sampling, free-energy estimation, and molecular design more efficient. To this end, I develop generative transport methods that learn mappings between molecular ensembles, with the goal of improving sampling efficiency and enabling accurate free-energy estimation. I also investigate how generative transport methods can support molecular design by coupling molecular generation with thermodynamic evaluation.

Since 2024, I have been pursuing a Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence at Ocean University of China.
